# Prompt 06: Quality review and revision

Reread as an editor, checking the actual body and research notes rather than self-declaring “above average.” Revise affected passages before delivery.

Apply 02-research.md's three independent acceptance checks to the integrated full text and actual evidence: valid information retained; important gaps resolved; added answers supported, relevant, nonredundant, and decision-useful. For new work without a prior draft, mark the retention baseline accordingly rather than inventing preservation coverage. Record specific new answers, changed judgments, evidence, body locations, and unresolved items in review.md. The checks cannot offset one another; new material cannot compensate for lost useful conditions or unsupported central conclusions.

The target is upper-moderate (中等偏上), not merely more complete-looking: key questions adequately answered, important facts supported, differences turned into concrete tradeoffs, and no core misleading conclusion. The LLM judges intent, evidence, feedback, reasoning, and full-text quality. Script success cannot certify any of those.

Check seven areas:

1. Is the trend only the entry point, with the body answering the target site's consumer question?
2. Are the title promise and all key questions from working notes resolved? Are comparison alternatives supported symmetrically, and can readers execute tutorials?
3. Relative to same-intent articles actually read, what concrete comparison, explanation, or method has been added, and what uncertainty does it remove? If this is only synthesis or rewriting, continue research.
4. Do image, URL, price, and attributes match the same product/variant? Are pros/cons supported without borrowing another product's properties?
5. Are any conclusions stronger than evidence, any tests invented, or unknowns treated as defects? Narrow claims where possible; flag missing evidence when core conclusions remain unsupported.
6. Is the same point expanded repeatedly across tables, cards, analysis, methods, and conclusions? Remove repetitions without losing unique conditions or evidence. Is the outline/product placement task-led, or another default comparison plus two cards? For batches, check substantive reasons for repeated structures without forcing superficial differences. Are internal retrieval tags useful, appropriately scoped, and distinct from verified product facts?
7. Are navigation, existing images, CTAs, mobile reading, attribution, and deliverables usable? Explicitly disclose unperformed checks. Optional new images do not gate body acceptance; mismatched images or nonexistent references do require handling.

Evidence of sufficient quality: readers can make a specific decision or take action; key conclusions are traceable; added value appears in the body; recommendations do not depend on invented products or generic adjectives. Complete structure and authoritative sources alone do not establish quality.

Classify gaps: preserve boundaries for unknown fields that do not affect key decisions; finish the body with products pending if no qualified item exists; mark research blocked when core comparison or procedural evidence is missing. Do not repeatedly dwell on research difficulty in the article.

Report body-quality assessment separately from delivery and technical checks. All draft delivery labels still require human review; content quality is not publication approval. For narrowly scoped corrections/deletions, respect scope and distinguish local completion from whole-article acceptance. If substantial defects remain, revise affected passages and reread their full-text context; do not lower the standard to declare success when evidence remains blocked.

Perform semantic review first, then run `scripts/check_article.py`. Static success does not verify remote links, images, or live indexing. Preview within tool capabilities; do not bypass access restrictions or claim visual acceptance without inspection.

Deliver English HTML and brief Chinese notes under local-contract.md. Do not default to the full article-review-sop scoring workflow, the older optimizer's five-sheet Excel format, site duplicate checks, or formal publication approval. If the user separately requests a dedicated review, use the appropriate skill for that task. New drafts always await human inspection: a topic marked “no human review required” is not permission to publish the resulting article without review.
