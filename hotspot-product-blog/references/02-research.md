# Prompt 02: Independent information-gain planning and research

Actually open and read the original hotspot sources to understand the event and writing direction, then find content answering the same consumer question. Do not write the article from one daily-report sentence, search snippets, or training memory alone.

Read enough relevant same-intent content to establish an existing-answer baseline; there is no source-count quota. If hotspot news does not answer the consumer question, it cannot be the sole benchmark. Initial sources and user suggestions are not the information ceiling: expansion must still serve the title promise, topic, and target reader. For a revision, also read the entire supplied draft and retained modules in context; its key claims are not automatically verified.

Information gain means doing research, explanation, and tradeoff work for readers so they can make a concrete judgment, resolve a question, or avoid a wrong choice. Basic intent coverage is only the floor.

## Independent prewriting task

Reader-task and content diagnosis → gain plan → targeted research → integrated writing → full-text review and gain acceptance. Do not expect proofreading to accomplish research incidentally or relabel every change as gain after writing.

Maintain separate working lists: defects (errors, omissions, contradictions, confusing explanations) and gain opportunities (deeper judgments worth completing even when existing information is correct).

Before drafting, record each worthwhile question:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Reader question | The specific understanding, choice, or action needed |
| Current coverage | What the draft or actually read sources already answer |
| Proposed answer | A concrete added judgment, initially a hypothesis to verify |
| Evidence needed | Required facts, use records, or specialist explanation |
| Body location | Where it belongs and how to avoid duplication |

Distinguish restatement, deeper analysis, and genuinely new answers. An already resolved question does not become new when retitled. Keep detailed working notes temporary; summarize outcomes in the existing review.md, not a new deliverable.

Qualifying information gain can include new relevant facts or evidence, and:

- A like-for-like comparison explaining how differences change a decision.
- The cause, counterexample, and limits of a common misconception.
- An executable method with key inputs, steps, checks, and next actions.
- Scenario-specific tradeoffs derived from real product fields.
- Explicit conditions for buying, avoiding, keeping what the reader owns, or not upgrading.

Target at least upper-moderate information gain (中等偏上): key questions adequately answered, important facts supported, differences translated into concrete tradeoffs, and no core misleading conclusion. More parameters, citations, headings, tables, changed wording, or changes made do not establish gain. Do not claim original tests, exclusive data, or superiority over all competing articles without evidence.

## Research by question, not quota

Insufficient initial material triggers targeted research, not automatic omission. Search engines may lead to same-intent articles, specialist media, original tests/videos, social posts, forums, retail reviews, and primary documents. Choose channels by the question; do not mandate every channel or fixed search, source, product, or word counts. Stop when the question has adequate evidence coverage.

- Specifications, compatibility, accessories, and after-sales conditions: prioritize official records for the exact model and market.
- Care/maintenance: read corresponding instructions, not advice transferred across materials or versions.
- Use performance: seek original tests or identifiable concrete use reports; repeatedly reading specifications is insufficient.
- Health, safety, and specialist conclusions: prioritize qualified institutions, standards, and relevant authoritative sources.
- Other editorial content: find omissions and useful reasoning; reliable original tests can support their measured findings. Do not copy prose or outlines.

Read the source itself. Search snippets, AI answers, AI review summaries, and model memory are leads, not evidence.

Record visited URLs, source roles, supported facts, retrieval dates, and applicable products/variants. Use official product pages or manuals for specifications and qualified specialist sources for principles. Media and communities can reveal questions, but shopping lists are not direct product verification. Support performance, safety, efficacy, and compatibility claims with appropriate primary or specialist evidence.

## Scope, independence, and feedback

Record source dates and conclusion-relevant conditions: exact model, size, version, material, color, region, use duration, load, and environment. Missing conditions limit inference. Separate direct facts or brand claims, third-party observations/tests/experiences, editorial analysis from verified facts, and explicitly identified hypotheses/calculation examples. Brand promotion is not independent testing; third-party experience is not this site's hands-on experience. Verify important retained claims as well as additions.

Syndicated copies of one report count as one source. Identify mixed-variant reviews, gifted products, employee/incentive relationships and limit their use. Resolve conflicting sources by checking entities, regions, dates, and conditions, not by selecting favorable findings.

Not every useful observation needs a controlled head-to-head test. Attribute a specific single-product report with its conditions. Cross-product comfort/durability rankings require evidence supporting that comparison. Lack of ranking evidence does not erase supported individual findings.

Actively investigate relevant pain points through original reviews, community discussion, long-term use, returns, and repair records for the exact product/scenario. Begin with neutral experience queries, then pursue identified issues; inspect contrary reports and possible condition differences. User-proposed scenarios are leads, not established facts. Do not manufacture consensus through negative-only searching.

The LLM distinguishes individual reports, recurring themes across independent reports, contradictory feedback, and reports whose model/conditions cannot be identified. Reposts, likes, and search rank are not independent users. Without a representative sample and denominator, do not infer prevalence or write “most,” “widespread,” or “many users.” Repetition alone does not establish a design defect or causation; absence of complaints does not prove absence of problems.

Every pain point used must become practical advice: who should care under what conditions; which choice/use behavior it affects; a specific pre-purchase screen, arrival check, or officially supported care/repair action; and when to accept the tradeoff, return/exchange, or choose differently. Do not merely list complaints or end with “maintain carefully/check yourself.” Feedback supports what someone encountered, not necessarily its cause or an effective remedy. Verify remedies separately; do not invent repairs, guarantee prevention, or infer warranty eligibility. If no remedy is supported, provide evidence-based avoidance/alternative conditions instead.

## Convert research into usable answers

Use facts → use implications → benefits/costs → conditions → concrete action internally, not as a repeated visible paragraph template. Value may come from integrating dispersed evidence and clarifying tradeoffs, not worldwide novelty. Never contradict facts to appear distinctive.

Allow conclusions such as “no upgrade needed,” “buy directly instead,” or “none of these candidates meets all requirements.” Compare prices in the same market/currency and comparable versions; separate required accessories from already-owned or unnecessary ones. Complete the research the article can do before assigning genuinely personal try-on/arrival checks. Returning all judgment work to the reader is not gain.

After reasonable targeted research, record unresolved important questions internally and narrow conclusions. Do not invent facts, experience, consensus, or remedies, or use “it depends” as a substitute for answers. Keep only decision-relevant limitations in the body, not research logs/audit commentary. Inability to establish a ranking does not block supported specific findings.

## Preservation and acceptance

For revisions, map every useful unique detail—accurate parameters, conditions, exceptions, steps, cases, and recommendation reasons—to its new location or a specific correction/deletion reason. New content cannot offset lost valid information; unchanged word count proves nothing. For new articles there is no duty to preserve all competitor content: retain relevant verified evidence and conditions needed for the article's own promised answers.

The LLM reads the integrated full text and actual evidence, separately judging: (1) valid information retained, or no prior-draft baseline for new work; (2) important gaps resolved; (3) added answers supported, nonredundant, relevant, and decision-useful. These cannot offset one another. Record the new answer, judgment changed, evidence, body location, and unresolved items in review.md. “More comprehensive/professional/helpful” is not acceptance evidence.

Intent recognition, evidence judgment, feedback synthesis, integration, and full-text acceptance belong to the LLM; scripts check only determinable file/structure/consistency properties. New images are optional, not a body-acceptance gate; prose must independently complete the task. Wrong existing images and references to nonexistent images still need handling. Narrow corrections/deletions do not authorize unrelated expansion; distinguish local task completion from whole-article quality.

If a public source is blocked, use relevant accessible alternatives without bypassing access controls. Preserve unresolved gaps and, when necessary, request material or login assistance from the user. Do not exaggerate research coverage. Heat does not need revalidation, but known errors or contradictions must not be copied into the body as facts.
